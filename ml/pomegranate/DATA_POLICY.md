# Dataset policy

Only datasets with verified dataset-level permissions may enter commercial training.

- Mendeley Fruit v1: CC BY 4.0 candidate
- Halabja Fruit v1: CC BY 4.0 candidate
- Time-series weather dataset: CC BY 4.0 candidate for contextual research
- Private or CC BY-NC research collections: do not train the commercial model unless permission is obtained

Record the exact source, version, license and checksum in the cloud audit before training.
